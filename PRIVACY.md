# Privacy Policy for PlayBound

**Last Updated:** March 29, 2026

At PlayBound, your privacy is critically important. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you use the PlayBound Discord bot, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide the core functionalities of the bot (economy, leaderboards, and game tracking), we collect the following data:
- **Discord User IDs:** Used to uniquely identify users across servers, track your global points, inventory, factions, and statistics.
- **Discord Server (Guild) IDs:** Used to store server-specific settings, custom shops, and leaderboard rankings.
- **Channel and Message IDs:** Temporarily stored for the duration of active game threads to facilitate games (like Serverdle or Trivia). We do not read or log messages that are not related to active bot interactions or commands.
- **Game Statistics:** Information like the number of games played, wins, current daily streaks, and points earned.

We **do not** collect personal real-world information, credit card data, or track your activity outside of the bot's direct games.

## 2. How We Use the Information
The data collected is used solely to:
- Maintain your persistent profile, economy points, and inventory.
- Track your ranking on global faction and server-specific leaderboards.
- Store configuration settings made by server administrators.
- Ensure the bot functions properly (like tracking active trivia matches).

## 3. Data Storage & Security
Data is stored securely in our database (MongoDB) and is only accessible by the core development team for maintenance, debugging, or support purposes.

## 4. Subscriptions and Payments
PlayBound offers Premium features through Discord's native Premium App Subscriptions. All billing, payment details, and subscription management are handled securely by Discord. We receive a notification from Discord when you subscribe, allowing us to grant your perks, but we **never** receive or store your financial information.

## 5. Data Deletion
If you wish to have your data permanently removed from our database, you can:
- Open a support ticket using the `/ticket` command.
- Join our official Support Server and request a manual data wipe.

If you are a server owner, removing the bot from your server will leave the server configuration in our database intact (so you don't lose settings if you re-invite it), but you may request a full wipe via our Support Server.

## 6. Updates to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available in our official repository or via our support channels.
